\section*{LLM Usage Statement}
\label{sec:llm}

A large language model (LLM) assistant was used in this work for writing, drafting and paraphrasing of the paper and its supplement, and for coding assistance with the analysis and figure scripts.
The authors designed the study, specified the harm definition, tolerances and decision rules, ran all experiments, verified every reported number against the result files, and revised and approved all text and code.
The LLM is not an author; the authors take full responsibility for the content, including any errors.
Only de-identified public datasets were used.
